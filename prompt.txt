Rewind City — an overcast afternoon, 1996

Create a dreamy, explorable American city district, inspired by Abyssal Garden.
It is a place, not a game: no goals, quests, characters, combat or collectibles.
Float through several blocks on a cloudy grey afternoon. Wet dark asphalt,
soft heavy fog, washed concrete, faded brick, dull teal and mustard. Interior
lights are already on. Fine drizzle drifts through the air; no heavy rain,
no hard sun shadows, no golden hour.

Remember a water tower, varied buildings and window grids, parapets and AC units,
telephone poles with sagging wires, streetlights, traffic signals, chain-link
fences and fictional billboards. REWIND VIDEO, Starlight Diner / EAT, the pink
and cyan INSERT COIN arcade alley, Groove Merchant records, a gas station,
and the Starlight Motel with a faded sign and empty pool. Empty swings sway
in the schoolyard beneath a basketball hoop. A striped parking lot and blank
drive-in screen wait for evening. Parked and slowly moving boxy 1990s cars,
headlights on; crows in the sky and an occasional distant plane.

Gentle floating controls with no gravity, building collision, district limits,
six meaningful viewpoints, desktop pointer lock, touch drift and look, a pause
menu and remembered detail settings. Subtle synthesized traffic, wind, siren,
school bell, train horn, crows and dripping. Let the visitor enter from an
overlay over an already visible city. Step outside. Nothing is asked of you.

Generate everything in code. Deliver one self-contained index.html, no external
requests, images, models, fonts or sound files. WebGL, never WebGPU. Works as a
local desktop file and over HTTP on desktop and mobile. Aim below 200 KB, reuse
geometry, limit distance with fog, cap pixel density and step down when slow.
Keep readable source, a Node build, and an invitation to make it your own.
